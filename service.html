Welcome to

Kelina Hospital

Kelina Hospital is a surgical hospital where a patient can have major surgery today and go home the same day, or less than 24 hours after surgery. Surgery is our primary focus at Kelina. Our theatres carry the latest equipment from the world's leading manufacturers, and foreign medical missions to Nigeria have consistently chosen us over other hospitals.

Mission

To promote, preserve and restore individual and family health by providing expert medical and surgical care within an innovative and dignified environment.

Objective

To be the leading centre for minimal access surgery in the region, adopting proven technology to shorten recovery time and reduce the burden of surgery on our patients.

Management and Staff

A team of experienced surgeons, nurses and support staff dedicated to attentive, respectful care at every stage of a patient's treatment.
