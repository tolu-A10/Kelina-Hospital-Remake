About Kelina Hospital

Excellence in Minimal Access Surgery and Patient Care

Who We Are

Kelina Hospital is a specialist surgical hospital focused on Endoscopic and Laser Surgery. We provide advanced medical care with modern technology and highly trained professionals. Kelina Hospital is a specialist surgical hospital dedicated to providing advanced medical and surgical care using modern, minimally invasive techniques. We focus on endoscopic and laser surgery, ensuring patients benefit from safer procedures, faster recovery, and reduced hospital stay. We are a team of highly trained medical professionals committed to excellence, innovation, and compassionate care. Our hospital is equipped with state-of-the-art technology that meets international standards, allowing us to deliver accurate diagnosis and effective treatment. At Kelina Hospital, we believe in putting patients first. We combine expertise, technology, and dedication to deliver healthcare services that improve lives and restore health with dignity.

Our Vision

To be a leading hospital in Africa delivering world-class minimally invasive surgical care with excellence, dignity, and innovation.Kelina Hospital envisions becoming a leading center of excellence in minimal access and advanced surgical care in Africa. We are committed to transforming healthcare delivery through innovation, modern medical technology, and highly skilled professionals. Our vision is to create a healthcare environment where patients receive safe, effective, and compassionate treatment with minimal recovery time. We aim to continuously improve surgical outcomes, expand access to specialized care, and set new standards in patient-centered healthcare services. Through dedication, integrity, and continuous advancement, Kelina Hospital strives to be a trusted name in healthcare—delivering world-class medical care that enhances lives and promotes long-term wellness.
